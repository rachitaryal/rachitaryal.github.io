Ten years of building software that has to hold together.

Software Architect

I design the shape of a system before it is built. How the parts depend on each other. How data moves. What must stay true as the product grows. A decade of taking unclear needs and turning them into applications a team can build, run, and keep.

  1. 01

    See the whole system.

    A product is more than its screens. I start with the dependencies, the data, and the decisions that will still matter in five years.

  2. 02

    Keep only the complexity the problem earns.

    Clear boundaries. A design no larger than the work it has to do.

  3. 03

    Leave something others can trust.

    A system is finished when it can be changed without fear.

If the problem is the system itself, start there.
